Odin PMS for Microsoft Teams

Odin PMS is the Teams app of Odin by Protect24x7, the rollout tracker a company runs on its own server. It sends each person who works on a rollout their Odin notifications (stage moves, approvals, tasks, mentions and alerts) as a card in their own chat, with a link back into Odin.

Privacy

  • The app only sends. It does not read your chats, files, calendar or presence, and it ignores anything posted to it.
  • To open your chat, Odin looks up your Microsoft 365 user id from your work email address, and keeps the chat's id with that address.
  • A card carries the same words as the notification in Odin's bell. Nothing is sent to any party other than Microsoft Teams.
  • The data stays in the company's own Odin, under the company's own policies. Questions go to your Odin administrator.

Terms of use

Odin PMS is provided by your company for its own staff, as part of Odin, and is used under your company's IT and acceptable-use policies. Switch the messages off for yourself on your Odin Account page ("Tell me about"), or ask an administrator to remove the app.
